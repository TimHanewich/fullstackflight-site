Full Stack Flight: Build Cohorts

Building a quadcopter from scratch is very doable, but it goes faster with someone in your corner. The FSF Build Cohort is a small, fully virtual program where you build and validate your own Centauri quadcopter, from bare parts to first flight, with direct guidance from the person who designed it.

  • ~8 participants in the pilot group
  • 10 weeks of guided building
  • 2 weekly sessions

What the pilot is expected to include

  • Live instructional videos, building together
  • Group troubleshooting for the problems everyone hits
  • Private flight-readiness reviews before you fly
  • Session recordings and supporting materials
  • A private community of people building the same thing

Who it's for

  • Engineering-focused makers with a passion for understanding
  • Some basic Python experience
  • Willing to learn soldering and hardware assembly
  • Able to set up a small electronics workspace
  • Access to a safe outdoor spot for flight testing

Components, tools, and 3D printing are purchased separately. Exact costs would be shared before enrollment.
