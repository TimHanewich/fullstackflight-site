Parts List

Everything needed to build the Centauri ecosystem, broken out by the two systems that make it up: the quadcopter itself and the pilot's transmitter.

Quadcopter

Core Electronics and Propulsion

  • 1x Raspberry Pi Pico
  • 1x MPU-6050 IMU
  • 1x HC-12 wireless transceiver
  • 1x LM2596 buck converter
  • 4x Readytosky 2300 KV brushless motors
  • 4x electronic speed controllers (ESCs)
  • 4x 5-inch tri-blade propellers, plus spares
  • 1x 4S LiPo battery
  • 1x female XT60 connector from this pack
  • 1x 100 kΩ resistor from the resistor pack
  • 1x 22 kΩ resistor from the resistor pack
  • 1x 10 µF capacitor
  • 1x 0.1 µF capacitor
  • 2x ferrite beads from the ferrite bead pack
  • 22-Gauge Electrical wire
  • Solder

3D-Printed Airframe

The complete airframe design files are available on Thingiverse. This uses approximately 164 g of PLA at 30% infill.

  • 1x lower plate
  • 1x upper plate
  • 4x arms
  • 1x MPU-6050 frame
  • 1x LM2596 frame
  • 1x GoPro mount (if mounting camera)
  • Antenna-mast components (3 pieces)

Mounting Hardware

Misc. hardware for assembling the quadcopter that you may already have lying around, and if not, can purchase at any local store.

  • Screws
    • M3 Screw Assortment Kit (for most structural connections)
    • M2*8mm pack (4 needed to secure RPi Pico)
  • 4-inch zip tie pack (for strapping down components/wires)
  • Double-sided foam tape for vibration-isolating the IMU

Radio Communication

  • 1x Taoglas TI.10.0111 433 MHz antenna
  • 1x 12-inch U.FL-to-SMA coaxial cable

Optional FPV Equipment

You can fly Centauri by eye (without any onboard cameras) if you want, and I did for a while! But if you want a "first person" perspective through an FPV monitor or goggles, you'll need these.

  • 1x AKK BA3 all-in-one FPV camera/transmitter
  • 1x JST-PH 2.0 connector
  • 1x SoloGood FPV monitor or compatible analog FPV goggles
  • 1x 3D-printed two-piece FPV protective frame

Transmitter

Electronic Components

All of the components required to build the transmitter's broadcasting capabilities.

  • 1x Raspberry Pi Pico
  • 1x HC-12 wireless transceiver
  • 1x CP2102 USB-to-UART adapter
  • 1x USB-compatible Xbox controller
  • 1x FLEXI-SMA-433 antenna
  • Any Windows or Linux laptop
  • Electrical wire
  • Solder

3D-Printed Platform

Small 3D-printed platform to mount the components to.

  • Transceiver platform (two-pieces)
  • PLA filament
