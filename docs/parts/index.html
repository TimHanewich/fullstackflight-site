Parts List

Everything needed to build the Centauri ecosystem, broken out by system: the quadcopter itself, the pilot's transmitter, and Link, a diagnostic tool for radio communication observability.

Quadcopter

Core Electronics and Propulsion

Electronics, motors, and power components required to control and fly the quadcopter.

  • 1x Centauri Flight Controller Carrier Board
  • 1x Raspberry Pi Pico
  • Header pin strips - for soldering the Raspberry Pi Pico directly to the Carrier Board
  • 1x MPU-6050 IMU
  • 1x HC-12 wireless transceiver
  • 1x 6-inch U.FL-to-SMA coaxial cable
  • 1x Taoglas TI.10.0111 433 MHz antenna
  • 1x MP1584EN 5V buck converter
  • 4x Readytosky 2300 KV brushless motors
  • 4x electronic speed controllers (ESCs)
  • 4x 5-inch tri-blade propellers, plus spares
  • 1x 4S LiPo battery
  • 1x female XT60 connector from this pack
  • 1x 100 kΩ resistor from the resistor pack
  • 1x 22 kΩ resistor from the resistor pack
  • 1x 10 µF capacitor
  • 1x 0.1 µF capacitor
  • 1x ferrite beads from the ferrite bead pack
  • 22-Gauge Electrical wire - for low-current and logic connections
  • 16-gauge electrical wire - for the main battery power leads, connecting the XT60 to the Carrier Board

3D-Printed Airframe

This uses approximately 155 grams of PLA at 30% infill.

  • PLA filament - for 3D printing the airframe
  • Complete airframe design files on Thingiverse
    • 1x lower plate
    • 1x upper plate
    • 4x arms
    • 1x MPU-6050 frame
    • 1x GoPro mount (if mounting camera)
    • Antenna-mast components (3 pieces)

Mounting Hardware

Misc. hardware for assembling the quadcopter that you may already have lying around, and if not, can purchase at any local store.

  • Screws
    • M3 Screw Assortment Kit
      • 4x M3×30mm - securing the 4 arms to the main body
      • 4x M3×8mm - securing motors (only if not included with motors)
      • 4x M3×8mm - securing GoPro mount to upper plate
      • 4x M3×12mm - securing MPU-6050 frame
      • 2x M3×16mm - antenna mast (bottom-to-arm and coupler-to-mast-top)
      • 3x M3×12mm - securing antenna base into mast coupler
    • M2 Screw Assortment Kit
      • 3x M2×8mm - securing Centauri Carrier Board to level 1
      • 1x M2×12mm - securing Centauri Carrier Board to level 1
  • 4-inch zip tie pack (for strapping down components/wires)
  • Double-sided foam tape for vibration-isolating the IMU
  • 1/2-inch Velcro strip (about 2 feet needed)

Optional FPV Equipment

You can fly Centauri by eye (without any onboard cameras) if you want, and I did for a while! But if you want a "first person" perspective through an FPV monitor or goggles, you'll need these.

  • 1x AKK BA3 all-in-one FPV camera/transmitter
  • 1x JST-PH 2.0 connector
  • 1x SoloGood FPV monitor or compatible analog FPV goggles
  • 1x 3D-printed two-piece FPV protective frame

Miscellaneous Tools

There is a good chance you have these on hand. But if not, here's what you'll need to get the work done and what I recommend.

  • Solder
  • Soldering iron - for soldering all of the electrical connections throughout the build
  • Balance charger - safely charges the LiPo battery, balancing voltage across its individual cells

Transmitter

Electronic Components

Components required to send the pilot's control inputs to the quadcopter.

  • 1x Raspberry Pi Pico
  • 1x HC-12 wireless transceiver
  • 1x CP2102 USB-to-UART adapter
  • 1x USB-compatible Xbox controller
  • 1x FLEXI-SMA-433 antenna
  • Any Windows or Linux laptop
  • Electrical wire
  • Solder

3D-Printed Platform

Small 3D-printed platform to mount the components to.

  • Transceiver platform (two-pieces)
  • PLA filament

Mounting Hardware

Screws for securing the transmitter's components and antenna to the 3D-printed platform.

  • Screws
    • M2 Screw Assortment Kit
      • 4x M2×8mm - securing RPi Pico to 3D-printed platform
    • M3 Screw Assortment Kit
      • 3x M3×12mm - securing antenna base into 3D-printed platform

Link

Electronic Components

All of the components required to build Link, a diagnostic tool for radio communication observability.

  • 1x Centauri Link Carrier Board
  • 1x Raspberry Pi Pico
  • Header pin strips - for soldering the Raspberry Pi Pico directly to the Carrier Board
  • 1x HC-12 wireless transceiver
  • 1x 6-inch U.FL-to-SMA coaxial cable
  • 1x Taoglas TI.10.0111 433 MHz antenna
  • 1x 5V boost regulator module
  • 1x 18650 battery
  • 1x TP4056 charge controller
  • 1x SSD-1306 OLED display
  • 1x Power button switch
  • 1x Push button
  • 1x 47 kΩ resistor from the resistor pack
  • 1x 100 kΩ resistor from the resistor pack
  • 22-Gauge Electrical wire - for low-current and logic connections

Mounting Hardware

Screws for securing Link's battery frame, OLED display, Carrier Board, and enclosure top plate.

  • Screws
    • M3 Screw Assortment Kit
      • 2x M3×12mm - securing 18650 battery frame
    • M2 Screw Assortment Kit
      • 4x M2×12mm - securing top plate
      • 4x M2×8mm - securing OLED display
      • 4x M2×12mm - securing Link Carrier Board to enclosure
