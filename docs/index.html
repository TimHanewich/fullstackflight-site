Build your own quadcopter ecosystem from scratch.

Full Stack Flight is an educational series on building a complete quadcopter ecosystem from scratch. It covers the full system, including the flight controller, airframe, transmitter, communication protocol, and telemetry analysis tooling, all built to be understandable, practical, and accessible to makers who want to learn how quadcopters actually work.

Meet Centauri

Centauri is the quadcopter system at the center of Full Stack Flight. It is a complete flight ecosystem built from scratch, including:

  • The Centauri Quadcopter
  • The Pilot's Transmitter
  • Wireless Communication Protocol
  • Tooling for unpacking and analyzing flight telemetry

My goal in designing Centauri was making the full system understandable, practical, and approachable by others.
