Learn how quadcopters actually work by building one from scratch.

Full Stack Flight is an educational series on building a quadcopter ecosystem from scratch: flight controller, airframe, transmitter, communication protocol, and telemetry tools, all custom and built entirely in Python! Made for aspiring engineers and curious makers who want more than a black box. Code, hardware, and explanations you can actually understand!

The Centauri Ecosystem

QUADCOPTER

The star of the show! A custom 3D-printed airframe with a Raspberry Pi Pico running a MicroPython flight controller. It reads pilot commands, senses motion, and adjusts all four motors through a 250 Hz PID loop to keep you flying.

TRANSMITTER

The pilot's control interface. A Python program reads an Xbox controller and sends commands through a Pico-powered HC-12 radio. A terminal dashboard shows inputs and pre-flight telemetry, with wireless PID updates for easier tuning.

LINK

Validate radio communication before taking flight. A small handheld device passively listens for Centauri radio communications. Check reception over distance, without risking your quadcopter on a long-range flight.

ANALYSIS

Turn flight data into understanding. A Python script decodes binary telemetry into readable CSV files, revealing battery voltage, motion, pilot inputs, and motor outputs. Plot flights, troubleshoot problems, or overlay telemetry on video.

Centauri is a flight ecosystem built from the ground up: a custom airframe and flight controller, a pilot's transmitter, a shared radio protocol, and tools for testing communications and analyzing flight data.

Together, they cover the whole journey, from pilot input to motor output to understanding what happened in the air.

My goal in designing Centauri was to make every part of it understandable, so anyone curious enough to look under the hood can learn how a real quadcopter works, and build one themselves.

Fully Open Source! Every part of Centauri is available for free on GitHub! Anyone is welcome to explore, learn, build, and collaborate.

Hi, I'm Tim!

I work in software by day and in my free time I love building crazy robotics projects and sharing them with like-minded people. Coming from the software world, I started in robotics with zero hardware know-how. Everything I know about motors, sensors, and flight came from teaching myself, piece by piece, mistake by mistake.

Building a quadcopter flight controller from scratch was my engineering Mount Everest for years. I finally got there in 2023 with Scout, and then rebuilt the whole thing, top to bottom, as Centauri.

Getting there was harder than it should have been. Nearly everything I found online was either a black-box product or a wall of complex mathematical theory. I built Full Stack Flight to share this the joy of robotics and flight with people like me! Curious makers that want to build and understand what they're building.

If you're building something, I'd genuinely love to hear about it. Come say hi in the Discord!
