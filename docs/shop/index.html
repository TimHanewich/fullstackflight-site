Centauri Flight Controller Carrier Board

+ shipping

The Centauri Flight Controller Carrier Board brings your Raspberry Pi Pico, IMU, radio receiver, and ESC control connections together in a clean, organized layout. It replaces messy point-to-point wiring while keeping the individual components accessible for learning and experimentation.

Your Pico still runs the MicroPython code that reads the sensors, interprets pilot commands, and tells the motors how to respond. The board connects the pieces without hiding what they do, so you can explore the code, tune the control loop, and understand what makes your quadcopter fly.

Includes one bare, unpopulated carrier PCB. Raspberry Pi Pico, sensors, radio, and other components sold separately. Assembly and soldering required.

Build your own window into Centauri’s radio communications. The Centauri Link Carrier Board brings the handheld Link monitor’s electronics together in an organized layout, simplifying assembly and reducing point-to-point wiring.

Once assembled, Link listens for Centauri radio traffic, helping you troubleshoot reception and check approximate range on foot before putting your drone in the air.

Includes one bare, unpopulated carrier board. Raspberry Pi Pico, radio module, display, battery, and other components are sold separately. Soldering, assembly, and a 3D-printed enclosure are required.
